# 🐐 Goat Paste

A strategic party game where two teams give clues to related mystery words, aiming to help their teammates while hindering opponents with "fuzzy" (not too good, but close) hints.

🎮 **[Play Now](https://dombash.github.io/goat-paste)** (Live on GitHub Pages!)

## Overview

Goat Paste is a web-based competitive word game that combines strategy, creativity, and quick thinking. Players work in teams to guess mystery words based on clues provided by their teammates, while simultaneously trying to confuse the opposing team with carefully crafted hints.

## How to Play

### Game Setup
- **Players**: 4+ players (2+ per team)
- **Rounds**: Multiple rounds of increasing difficulty

### Rules

1. **The Mystery Words**: Each round, players are given a set of mystery words they need to guess.

2. **Giving Clues**: On your turn, your team gives clues to help your teammates guess the words. But here's the catch—the other team can also hear your clues!

3. **The Strategy**: The goal is to give clues that are:
   - **Good enough** for your team to understand
   - **Fuzzy enough** that the other team can't capitalize on them
   - **Related** to the mystery word without being too obvious

4. **Guessing**: Teams guess which word matches the clue. First team to correctly guess gets the point!

5. **Winning**: The team with the most points after all rounds wins!

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- No installation required!

### Running the Game

1. Clone the repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/DomBash/goat-paste.git
   cd goat-paste
   ```

2. Open `index.html` in your web browser:
   ```bash
   open index.html
   ```
   Or simply double-click the file.

3. Gather your players and start playing!

## Game Strategy Tips

- **For Clue-Givers**: Think about what your team knows vs. what the other team might infer
- **For Guessers**: Pay attention to tone, hesitation, and emphasis—they're clues to the clue!
- **Team Coordination**: Establish subtle communication patterns (without violating game rules!)
